Can't settle even when you're tired? Even with a calmer backdrop today, some people may still feel that thin, papery restlessness at the edges. Space weather is quiet—no disruptive solar bursts pointing our way—so the body’s wind‑down cues have a cleaner lane to line up. If you’re still tossing, that can be leftover momentum from recent days or from local stuff like heat, pressure swings, or allergens. Try turning lights warm and low after sunset and pick one simple, repeatable cue—like a five‑minute stretch or a short dusk walk—to tell your system, “we’re closing up for the night.” Earlier caffeine cutoffs help that message land. Leave a little blank space around bedtime (no last‑minute tasks) so your nervous system has time to downshift.
