## What is deliberately not a new opportunity

Default toward a viable implementation path for proposed improvements. Additional charts, feeds, research or AI capabilities should have a useful purpose, bounded scope and appropriate validation; lack of immediate monetization alone is not a rejection reason. Preserve the established shared architecture and avoid duplicating work. The most important recovered opportunities combine scalable acquisition/engagement, reduced founder workload, useful product improvements and scientifically sound long-term capability.
